# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-led community. Be constructive, distinguish
people from ideas, disclose conflicts, respect privacy, and avoid vendor disparagement. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate misuse of security information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on
[@gregorsiwinski's GitHub profile](https://github.com/gregorsiwinski). Maintainers may warn, restrict,
or remove participants and will avoid conflicts of interest when handling reports.
